- AI-integrated degrees: cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence is woven into UG and PG programmes across departments to build tomorrow's tech leaders.

- Free UPSC Civil Services Examination coaching at no extra cost for interested students, through the Centre for Competitive Examination.
- Special coaching for competitive exams: CA, CSIR-NET, UGC-NET, GATE and more for final-year UG/PG students.

- Emerging tech skill courses: hands-on training in LED/Solar technology, and industry-integrated programmes with IBM and NASSCOM.
- Safe, secure, drug and ragging-free campus: dedicated women's hostels with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, a strict zero-tolerance policy on drugs and ragging, anti-ragging committees, and NCC/NSS initiatives.
- Exceptional placement focus: the University is committed to securing career opportunities not just for professional programmes (B.Tech, BBA, MBA) but for all eligible UG and PG students.
- Award-winning researchers and international/national funded research projects across departments.
- High quality education at an affordable cost, under the motto "Value Based & Affordable Education."

## Infrastructure
Modern labs and classrooms across all science and engineering departments, with smart classrooms and interactive boards.
Central Library: large collection of books, journals and periodicals, dedicated reading and study spaces.
Sports & Gymnasium: cricket ground, modern gym equipment, indoor and outdoor sports facilities for a healthy campus life.

## Hostel and Transport
Separate, fully furnished hostels for boys and girls, with a homely and safe environment.
Hostels are drug-free, with caring wardens and 24x7 security with CCTV cameras.
Facilities include hostel mess with nutritious vegetarian food, air-conditioned reading rooms, an indoor gaming hall, and an outdoor gym.
Transport network: over 100 KM of bus routes, connecting more than 275 cities and villages to the campus, so day-scholar students have wide transport coverage.

## Unique Free Program — Sri Guru Granth Sahib Studies

B.A. (Hons.) in Sri Guru Granth Sahib Studies is a unique 4-year program — probably the first of its kind offered by any university in India or abroad. This program is fully residential and completely FREE — no tuition fee, no boarding charges, no lodging charges. It covers complete study of the Holy Scripture, Guru Darbar Maryada, Sikh history, and comparative study of world religions.
