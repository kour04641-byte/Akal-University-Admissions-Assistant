## University Fee Structure (Session 2026-27) — Source: Official Prospectus 2026-27

All figures are in Indian Rupees (₹), as per the official Akal University Prospectus for session 2026-27. These are University-level charges; please always confirm the final payable amount with the Admission Office before paying, as the University reserves the right to revise fees up to 15% annually.

- Prospectus Fee: ₹500 (non-refundable)
- Admission Fee: ₹5,000 (non-refundable)

## Other Charges (apply on top of tuition, paid every semester/year — see hostel file for hostel-specific charges)
- Examination Fee: ₹4,000 per semester (Science & Technology programs) / ₹3,500 per semester (Non-Science programs)
- Life Time Alumni Registration: ₹1,000 (one-time, non-refundable, charged in 1st year only)
- Annual Medical Insurance: ₹400 per year
- Electricity (in hostel): charged as per actual consumption

## Late Fee for Semester Fee Payment
- Up to 15 days late: ₹200
- Up to 30 days late: ₹500
- Up to 59 days late: ₹1,000
- On the 60th day: name struck off the rolls (re-admission with ₹2,000 fee is needed, within 15 days, subject to approval)

## How to Pay
Official online fee payment link: https://auts.ac.in/facilities/fee-payment/
For any fee-related query, confirmation, or bank payment details, please contact the Admission Office / Accounts Branch directly — for security reasons this assistant does not share bank account numbers.

## Fee Revision & Refund
- The University reserves the right to increase fees by up to 15% annually.
- Fee refunds follow UGC Guidelines.
- SC/ST students have a separate dropout fee policy as per government norms.
