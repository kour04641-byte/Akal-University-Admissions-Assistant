Fees are listed per semester and per year. The university website has a Fee Payment page where students select their year and semester.
The instalment schedule, due dates, refund and cancellation policy are not available in this chatbot. For these, contact the Admission Office on 1800-2020-100 or admission@auts.ac.in.
The tuition fee listed above is separate from hostel, mess, electricity and transport charges. Scholarship benefits apply on the tuition fee of the selected programme.
