## Co-Curricular & Extracurricular Life — Source: Official Prospectus 2026-27

- Leadership: mentorship programs, personality development workshops, leadership roles in department/academic clubs
- Internships: tie-ups across industries for internships in every course offered
- Akal Incubation Centre: mentorship, working space, testing lab and support for student start-ups and passion projects
- Community Outreach: NSS Cell, Red Ribbon Club, NCC, Gurmat Camps, OPD and Aukhad volunteer activities
- Academic Clubs: in every department — covering visual arts, painting, drawing, photography, graphic design, poetry
- Sports & Culture: sports, athletics, cultural and heritage games, indoor games held every year
- Educational Excursions: organized by every department to places of educational/historical importance
- Summer Training Camps: intensive June–July training camps run by various departments
- Alumni Meet: annual reconnection event for alumni, current students and faculty
