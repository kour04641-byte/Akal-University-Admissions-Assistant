## Ph.D Admission 2026-27 — Source: Official Announcement (auts.ac.in)

- Last date to apply: 11 July 2026
- Written Entrance Test: 19 July 2026 (Sunday)
- Result declaration: 25 July 2026
- Last date for Ph.D registration (for qualified candidates): 10 August 2026
- Note: These dates are subject to change by the University — please confirm current dates with the Admission Office before applying, especially if today's date is close to or past these dates.

## Disciplines Offered (Regular and Part-Time both available)
Chemistry, Commerce, Computer Science, Economics, Education, English, Mathematics, Physics, Punjabi, Management, Political Science, Zoology, and History.
Note: Seats per department depend on supervisor availability and specialization — exact seat numbers are not published; please confirm with the Admission Office.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Master's degree (or equivalent) with at least 55% marks, or Grade B on the UGC 7-point scale.
- Relaxation to 50% marks for SC/ST/OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) candidates, differently-abled candidates, and those who completed their Master's before 19 September 1991.

## Entrance Test
- Called the "Written Entrance Test" — conducted at Akal University Campus, Talwandi Sabo.
- Paper I: Objective (MCQ) — 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM
- Paper II: Subjective — 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM
- The test is qualifying in nature: 50% marks needed to qualify (45% for reserved categories).
- Full syllabus is available as a downloadable PDF on the official website — please check auts.ac.in or contact the Admission Office for the exact syllabus document.

## Exemption from Written Test
Candidates holding UGC-NET, CSIR-NET/JRF, SLET, GATE, JEST, or other national-level scholarship/fellowship qualifying tests are EXEMPT from the written entrance test — but they must still attend the interview.

## Admission Process
1. Written Entrance Test (qualifying — except for exempted candidates above)
2. Interview — candidate presents their research interest before a Selection Committee

## Part-Time Ph.D
Part-time Ph.D is permitted, but the candidate must submit an employer's NOC (No Objection Certificate) confirming: permission to study part-time, sufficient time available for research, and relief from duty for coursework if required.

## Application Fee
- Without NET qualification: ₹1,500
- NET qualified: ₹1,000
- Payment: Please pay only through the University's official payment channel/link, or contact the Admission Office directly for the correct, verified payment details — for security, exact bank account details are not shared here. After payment, attach the UPI/UTR transaction screenshot (if paid online) or the original fee receipt (if paid offline) with your application form.

## How to Apply
1. Download the Ph.D application form from the official website (auts.ac.in).
2. Fill in all details, including academic records from 10th standard onward.
3. Attach self-attested photocopies of: 10th certificate, 10+2 certificate, graduation certificate, post-graduation certificate, M.Phil certificate (if any), and NET/GATE certificate (if applicable).
4. Attach proof of fee payment.
5. Email the scanned form and all documents to: adres@auts.ac.in
