## Admission Process & Eligibility (Session 2026-27) — Source: Official Prospectus 2026-27

## Eligibility Criteria
- Under-Graduate programs: minimum 60% marks in 10+2 or equivalent examination.
- Post-Graduate programs: minimum 60% marks in graduation or equivalent examination.
- SC/ST candidates and Persons with Disability (PWD, with at least 40% physical disability certificate) get a 5% relaxation in the minimum aggregate percentage.
- B.A.-B.Ed (ITEP) and Physiotherapy (BPT) admissions follow the eligibility criteria set by their respective regulatory bodies (NCTE/university norms for ITEP, PCI/relevant council for BPT) — please confirm current year specifics with the Admission Office.
- Additional 5% seats (over and above total seats in each program) are reserved for NRI/PIO/Foreign Nationals.
- Reservation for SC/ST/OBC/BC/PWD follows the Government of Punjab reservation policy.

## How to Apply — Step by Step
1. Fill the Admission Form completely and sign it, attaching all prescribed documents. A separate form is needed for each program if applying to more than one.
2. Admission is purely on merit basis; only candidates meeting the minimum eligibility criteria are considered for the merit list.
3. If applying under a reservation category, the category must be mentioned in the form and proof attached before the last date — otherwise the candidate is automatically considered under the general category.
4. Submit the complete form (in person, or by registered/speed post) before the due date to: Admission Cell, Akal University, Talwandi Sabo, Bathinda – 151302 (Punjab). The University is not responsible for postal delay or loss in transit.
5. Incomplete applications (missing documents, etc.) may not be considered.
6. Apply online here: https://auts.ac.in/au-online-registration/

## Documents to Attach with the Admission Form

General Category applicants need:
10th DMC photocopy, 12th DMC photocopy, self Aadhaar card photocopy (self-attested), father's Aadhaar card photocopy (self-attested), mother's Aadhaar card photocopy (self-attested), 4 passport photographs, original Character Certificate, original Migration Certificate, Gap year Affidavit (if applicable).

SC Category applicants need all of the above, plus: original Income Certificate from Tehsildar (issued after 31 March), Caste Certificate photocopy from Tehsildar, Punjab Residence Certificate photocopy, student bank account photocopy, OTR (One Time Registration) Number, Aadhaar-linked bank account with IFSC code.

## Change of Program
A student who wants to change their program must apply to the Admission Coordinator and deposit ₹5,000, subject to: seat availability in the new program, meeting its eligibility criteria, and applying within 20 days of the session's commencement along with parent/guardian consent in person. No change is permitted after 20 days of session commencement.

## Academic Calendar (Session 2026-27)
Odd Semester: Classes commence 13 July 2026; teaching runs 13 July – 30 November 2026 (99 days); end-semester exams 7–24 December 2026; winter break 25–31 December 2026.
Even Semester: Classes commence 1 January 2027; teaching runs 1 January – 12 May 2027 (90 days); end-semester exams 17 May – 4 June 2027.
Total teaching days for the year: 189. This calendar is not applicable to B.A.-B.Ed and MBA programs, and is subject to change by the University.

## General Academic Guidelines
- 75% attendance (counted separately for theory and practical in each paper) is compulsory for every student to be allowed to appear in exams.
- Minimum C grade is required in both internal assessment and external evaluation to pass a course.
- Students get a total of 3 chances (1 regular attempt + 2 reappear attempts) to clear a subject/paper.
- A full-time student cannot hold a full-time job simultaneously (exception: those on approved study leave from an employer).
- Separate seating arrangements for boys and girls are followed in and outside classrooms as prescribed by the University.
