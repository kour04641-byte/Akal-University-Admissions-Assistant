## Eligibility criteria - Undergraduate (UG) programmes
A minimum of 60% marks in 10+2 or an equivalent examination is required for most UG programmes. For SC/ST candidates and persons with disability, 55% marks.
Special requirements: B.Tech needs the Non-Medical stream; BPT needs PCB with 50% and NEET; B.Sc. Physics needs Physics and B.Sc. Mathematics needs Mathematics in 10+2.

## Eligibility criteria - Postgraduate (PG) programmes
A minimum of 60% marks in graduation or an equivalent examination is required. For SC/ST candidates and persons with disability, 55% marks. Some programmes need a related subject at graduation level.

## Relaxation in eligibility marks
SC/ST candidates and persons with disability get a 5% relaxation in the aggregate percentage. Persons with disability must produce a medical certificate showing at least 40% physical disability.

## How to apply for admission (UG and PG programmes)
Register and apply online at https://auts.ac.in/au-online-registration/
A separate form must be filled for each course. Admission is on merit. Incomplete applications or applications without the required documents are not considered.
For help, call the Admission Helpline 1800-2020-100 (toll-free) or email admission@auts.ac.in.

## Documents required with the admission form
- Self-attested copy of the Matriculation (10th) or equivalent certificate showing date of birth
- Self-attested copy of the detailed marks card of the qualifying examination, or proof of having appeared in it
- Character certificate issued by the Head Master or Principal of the school or college last attended
- Recent passport-size photographs
The full and current list is on the university's admission page.

## Change of course or stream
A student who wants to change the stream or specialization, or move from one course to another, must apply to the Admission Coordinator and deposit the required fee. The change is allowed subject to the university's conditions.
