## Department-wise Programs, Research Areas & Career Paths — Source: Official Prospectus 2026-27

## Faculty of Sciences

**Department of Physics**
Programs: B.Sc.(Hons.), B.Sc.(Non-Medical), B.Sc.(Hons. with Research), M.Sc., Ph.D
International collaborations: IPGP-CNRS (France), Rutgers University (USA), HBCSE-TIFR Mumbai, IIT Roorkee, VECC Kolkata, Panjab University
Research areas: Energy Harvesting, Polymeric Shielding & Nano Materials; Theoretical Nuclear Physics; Glasses for Functional Applications; Quantum Optics, Quantum Information & Quantum Machine Learning
Careers: Professor/TGT/PGT Teacher; Scientist at DRDO, ISRO, BARC, ONGC, BHEL; Radiation Physicist; Data Scientist

**Department of Chemistry**
Programs: B.Sc.(Hons.), B.Sc.(Hons. with Research), M.Sc., Ph.D
Research areas: Organic, Inorganic, Physical & Analytical Chemistry; Green Chemistry and Sustainable Materials; Environmental Chemistry and Wastewater Treatment; Nanoscience and Nanotechnology; Polymer Chemistry and Material Science
Careers: Professor/TGT/PGT; Pharmaceutical & Chemical Industries; Forensic Labs; ONGC, IOCL, Pollution Control Boards; Food Safety Officer

**Department of Mathematics**
Programs: B.Sc.(Hons.), B.Sc.(Hons. with Research), M.Sc., Ph.D
Highlights: 100% placement for eligible PG students; strong national & international collaborations
Research areas: Group Theory, Number Theory, Fuzzy Optimization, Ordinary/Fractional/Non-Linear Differential Equations
Careers: Professor/Researcher/TGT/PGT; Scientist at DRDO, ISRO; Data Analyst, Mathematical Modelling & Computational Expert, Banking

**Department of Life Sciences**
Programs: B.Sc.(Hons.), B.Sc.(Hons. with Research), M.Sc.(Zoology), Ph.D
Special training: Apiculture, Vermi-composting, Aquarium Fish Keeping, Molecular Biology techniques
Research areas: Entomology & Pest Management; Animal Physiology & Biochemistry; Molecular Biology, Aquatic Biology & Fisheries; Parasitology & Immunology
Careers: Professor/TGT/PGT; Research Scientist; Infection Control Specialist, Medical Technologist, Fishery Officer, Wildlife Conservationist, Embryologist, Ecologist

**Department of Physiotherapy**
Program: Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) — 5 years (4 years + 1 year compulsory internship)
Highlights: Free Physiotherapy OPD, ultra-modern physiotherapy facilities, physiotherapy camps in rural/urban areas
Research areas: Orthopaedics, Neurology, Cardiopulmonary, Pediatrics, Geriatrics, Women's Health & Sports
Careers: Professor/Researcher/Lecturer; Rehabilitation Specialist; Cardiopulmonary/Geriatric/Pediatric Physiotherapist

## Faculty of Engineering & Technology

**Department of Computer Science & Engineering**
Programs: B.Tech.(CSE) Data Science / AI-ML / Cyber Security; BCA(Hons.) Data Analytics / AI-ML / Graphic Design & Web Development; MCA; Ph.D
Research areas: Machine Learning & AI, Agentic AI & Generative AI, Blockchain & Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, Software Development
Careers: Software Engineer, Network Engineer, System Administrator, IT Project Manager, AI Specialist, Robotics Engineer, Blockchain Developer, Cloud Architect, Cyber Analyst, Data/Information Scientist; Armed Forces Technical Entry

## Faculty of Humanities & Social Sciences

**Department of Education**
Program: B.A.-B.Ed (4-Year Integrated ITEP), Ph.D
Features: ITEP curriculum, constructivist learning, ICT-enabled classrooms, inclusive education

**Department of Social Science**
Programs: B.A.(Hons.), B.A.(Hons. with Research), M.A., Ph.D
Special focus: Competitive exams like IAS, IPS, IFS
Research areas: Great-Power Politics, India's Foreign Policy, South Asian Security Dynamics, State Politics, Poverty & Welfare Studies, Modern/Medieval Indian History
Careers: Professor/TGT/PGT; Political Consultant/Analyst/Advisor; Policy Analyst; Archaeologist; Archivist

**Department of Economics**
Programs: B.A.(Hons.), B.A.(Hons. with Research), M.A., Ph.D
Research areas: Agricultural & Health Economics, Climate Change and Health, Development & Ecological Economics, Migration and Development, International Trade
Careers: Professor/Researcher/TGT/PGT; Business/Data Analyst; Economics & Financial Consultant; Indian Economics Services; Policy Advisor

**Department of Psychology**
Programs: B.A.(Hons.), B.A.(Hons. with Research), M.A., Post Graduate Diploma in Guidance & Counselling, Ph.D
Features: Personal counseling for student mental well-being, hands-on psychological assessment tools
Research areas: Clinical, Cognitive, Industrial, Developmental, Sports, Health, Positive, Environmental & Military Psychology; Guidance & Counseling; Stress Management
Careers: Professor/Researcher/TGT/PGT/Counselor; Psychologist, Forensic Psychologist, Neuropsychologist, Child Psychologist; De-addiction Centers

**Department of Liberal Arts (Punjabi Medium)**
Programs: B.A.(Hons.), B.A.(Hons. with Research), Ph.D
Research areas: Social/Political/World History, Comparative Politics, International Relations, Punjabi Language & Linguistics, Punjabi Literature, Punjabi Culture & Society, History of Punjab, Punjabi Folklore
Careers: Professor, School Teacher, Academic Counselor; Cultural Officer, Tourism Officer, Heritage Manager; UPSC Civil Services, State PSC; Archaeologist, Museum Curator, Archivist

**Department of English Studies**
Programs: B.A.(Hons.), B.A.(Hons. with Research), M.A., Ph.D
Research areas: English Language & Literature, Cultural Studies, Marginalised Literature, Effective Communication Skills
Careers: Professors/Teachers/Researchers; Editorial & Publishing; Content Strategy & Copywriting; Public Speaking; strategic campus placement cell support

**Punjabi Department (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਵਿਭਾਗ)**
Programs: B.A.(Hons.), B.A.(Hons. with Research), M.A.(Hons.), Ph.D
Focus: Punjabi language, literature & culture; Gurbani grammar; history of Punjabi language and Gurmukhi script; translation (English-Hindi-Punjabi)
Careers: Teaching (school/college/university); journalism & media; translation; script writing; literary writing

**Department of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Studies (ਸ੍ਰੀ ਗੁਰੂ ਗ੍ਰੰਥ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਸਟੱਡੀਜ਼ ਵਿਭਾਗ)**
Programs: B.A.(Hons.) — 4 years, fully residential and COMPLETELY FREE (no tuition, no boarding/lodging charges — probably the first such program by any university in India or abroad); also Certificate Courses
Covers: Complete recitation (Sampuran Sanjhiya) of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji, Sikh history, comparative study of religions, Katha and Vyakhyan (religious discourse)
Careers: Religious teacher; Sikh preacher (Granthi), Kirtan performer (Ragi/Kirtaniya); jobs in religious organizations; Government — NCC in the Army

## Faculty of Management & Commerce

**Department of Management**
Programs: BBA(Hons.) Business Analytics, MBA Data Analytics, Ph.D
Highlight: MBA offers a 1+1 international arrangement in collaboration with Virginia Commonwealth University (USA) and University of North Carolina (USA)
Research areas: Industry 4.0 & 5.0, Digital Transformation, Consumer Behavior & Sustainability, specialization in Marketing/Finance/HR
Careers: Business Intelligence Analyst, Marketing Analyst, Management Consultant, Finance/Risk Analyst, E-commerce & Social Media Analyst; Management Trainee roles in PSUs (Finance, HR, Marketing, Operations)

**Department of Commerce**
Programs: B.Com.(Hons.), B.Com.(Hons.)+CA, M.Com., Ph.D
Research areas: Accounting, Finance & Management; Marketing, HRM, Entrepreneurship; E-Commerce, Digital & International Business; Banking, Insurance, Taxation
Careers: Professor/Lecturer/School Teacher; Accounting & Auditing, Income Tax/Customs, Tax Analyst, GST Consultant; Banking & Insurance; Stock Market; Professional routes — CA/CS/CWA/CMA/CFA
