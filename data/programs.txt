## Akal University - programme areas
Akal University, Talwandi Sabo offers UG (honours), PG and Ph.D. programmes in these streams:
- Humanities and Social Sciences: Economics, Education, English, Psychology, Social Science, Punjabi, Liberal Arts, Sri Guru Granth Sahib Studies
- Sciences: Chemistry, Mathematics, Life Sciences (Botany, Medical), Physics, Zoology
- Engineering and Technology: Computer Science and Engineering (B.Tech), Computer Applications (BCA, MCA)
- Management: Commerce and Business Administration (Akal Business School)
- Paramedical Sciences: Physiotherapy, Medical Lab Technology
Most programmes follow the semester system and are honours-based.

## Humanities and Social Sciences programmes - duration and eligibility
- B.A. (Hons.) Economics: 4 years, 10+2 with 60% marks. M.A. (Hons.) Economics: 2 years, graduation with 60%. Ph.D. Economics: 3 years.
- B.A. (Hons.) English: 4 years, 10+2 with 60%. M.A. (Hons.) English: 2 years, graduation with 60% or any degree with at least 4 English papers. Ph.D. English: 3 years.
- B.A. (Hons.) Psychology: 4 years, 10+2 with 60%. M.A. (Hons.) Psychology: 2 years, B.Sc. with Psychology as a subject and 60%. Ph.D. Psychology: 3 years.
- B.A. (Hons.) Social Science: 4 years, 10+2 with 60%. Ph.D. in History and Political Science: 3 years. M.A. History, Political Science and Punjabi are also offered at PG level.
- Integrated B.A. B.Ed.: 4 years, 10+2 with 60% (55% for SC/ST and persons with disability).
- B.A. (Hons.) Sri Guru Granth Sahib Studies: 3 years, 10+2 with 60% (55% for reserved categories). Certificate Course in Sri Guru Granth Sahib Studies: 1 year, only for students already enrolled in the university.
- B.A. (Hons.) Punjabi and B.A. (Hons.) Liberal Arts (Punjabi medium) are also offered.

## Sciences programmes - duration and eligibility
- B.Sc. (Hons.) Chemistry: 4 years, 10+2 with 60%. M.Sc. (Hons.) Chemistry: 2 years, B.Sc. with Chemistry and 60%. Ph.D. Chemistry: 3 years.
- B.Sc. (Hons.) Mathematics: 4 years, 10+2 with Mathematics and 60%. M.Sc. (Hons.) Mathematics: 2 years, B.Sc. with Mathematics and 60%. Ph.D. Mathematics: 3 years.
- B.Sc. (Hons.) Physics: 3 years, 10+2 with Physics and 60%. M.Sc. (Hons.) Physics: 2 years, B.Sc. with Physics and 60%. Ph.D. Physics: 3 years.
- B.Sc. Medical (Life Sciences): 3 years, 10+2 with 60% (55% for reserved categories). M.Sc. Botany: 2 years, B.Sc. Medical with 60% (55% reserved). Ph.D. Botany: 3 years (Master's with 60%, or exemption via CSIR-NET/GATE).
- M.Sc. Zoology is also offered at PG level.

## Engineering, Computer Applications, Commerce and Management programmes
- B.Tech (Hons.) Computer Science and Engineering (specialisations: AI-ML, Data Science, Cyber Security): 4 years, 10+2 Non-Medical with 60% (55% for reserved categories).
- BCA (Hons.) (AI-ML, Data Analytics, Graphic Design and Web Development): 3 years, 10+2 in any stream with 60% (55% for reserved categories). Master of Computer Applications (MCA) is offered at PG level.
- B.Com (Hons.): 4 years, 10+2 with 60%. B.Com (Hons.) + CA is also offered. M.Com (Hons.): 2 years, B.Com with 60%. Ph.D. Commerce: 3 years.
- BBA (Hons.) Business Analytics: 3 years, 10+2 with 60% (55% for reserved categories). MBA - Data Analytics is offered at PG level.

## Paramedical programmes
- Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT): 4 years plus 1 year compulsory internship. Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology (PCB) with 50% marks; appearing in NEET is mandatory.
- B.Sc. Medical Lab Technology (MLT): 4 years, 10+2 with 60% marks (55% for reserved categories).
- PG Diploma in Guidance and Counselling is also offered.

## Ph.D. programme - disciplines
Ph.D. seats are offered in 13 subjects: Chemistry, Commerce, Computer Science, Economics, Education, English, Mathematics, Physics, Punjabi, Management, Political Science, Zoology and History. The number of seats in each department depends on supervisor availability and specialization.
