Students from Botany, Chemistry, Zoology, Physics and Mathematics departments have regularly qualified CSIR-NET/JRF, GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering), and JAM (Joint Admission Test for Masters), confirming the strength of the Sciences faculty.

These results show that Akal University students go on to top international universities (USA, Canada, Italy), clear India's toughest competitive exams (UGC-NET, CSIR-NET, GATE, CTET), and get selected for roles in top companies and research institutes, supported by the University's mentorship-based teaching and free competitive-exam coaching.
