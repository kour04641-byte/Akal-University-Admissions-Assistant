## About Akal University
Akal University, Talwandi Sabo (district Bathinda, Punjab) was founded in 2015 by the Kalgidhar Society with a vision of accessible, value-based education rooted in Sikh principles of integrity and selfless service. The university says it is a State University established by a Punjab Act and is UGC recognised. Teaching is in English medium, and programmes follow the semester and choice based credit system.

## Campus and facilities
The campus has a digital library, advanced research labs, smart classrooms, seminar and conference halls, a modern gymnasium, a sports complex, a health centre, boys' and girls' hostels with a vegetarian mess, and an on-campus Darbar Sahib. The university describes its campus as drug-free with 24x7 security and CCTV, and has a women's hostel with controlled access.

## Placements
The university lists Oracle India, Airtel, KC Global Tech, Xenage Solution, The Kalgidhar Trust and others among the organisations where its graduates work. For placement details, contact the Admission Office.
