## About Akal University — Official Facts

## Key Facts
- Name: Akal University
- Location: Talwandi Sabo, District Bathinda, Punjab
- Legal Status: State private university under the Akal University Act, 2015 (Punjab Act No. 25 of 2015); listed by UGC under Section 2(f)
- Inaugurated: 17 July 2015 (currently in its 12th academic session, 2026-27)
- Sponsoring Body: The Kalgidhar Society, Baru Sahib (Himachal Pradesh)
- Campus Type: Rural, residential
- Levels of Programmes: UG, PG and Ph.D.
- Medium of Instruction: English
- Statutory Bodies: Governing Body, Board of Management, Academic Council, Finance Committee

## Heritage & Identity
Akal University is located at the historic site where, three centuries ago, the tenth Sikh Master Shri Guru Gobind Singh Ji stayed at Talwandi Sabo and finalized the compilation of Guru Granth Sahib Ji — prophesying this place would become "Guru Ki Kashi," a great centre of learning. About two centuries later, Sant Attar Singh Ji Maharaj established Gurumat Vidyalaya here as a first step, and about a century after that, Akal University was founded under the leadership of Padma Shree Late Baba Iqbal Singh Ji (Baru Sahib Wale).

## Vision
To become a 'Centre of Excellence' at par with the best universities in India and the world, committed to holistic development of students through values-based, quality and ethical education — building core competencies in communication, critical thinking, reasoning, and the capacity to work across cultures, languages and traditions.

## Mission
"To establish permanent peace in the world through value-based scientific education and spiritual rejuvenation, creating good global citizens."

## Core Values
- Value-based, quality education: academic rigour joined with ethics, humility and service (Sewa)
- Innovation and excellence in teaching, research and administration
- Meritocracy and integrity: fair, transparent, merit-driven decisions
- Accountability and social responsibility to students, parents, society and regulators
- Equity and inclusiveness: access for rural, first-generation and economically weaker learners

## The Kalgidhar Society Network
Akal University is backed by The Kalgidhar Society, Baru Sahib — a non-profit charitable organization working in education, healthcare, de-addiction, and women's empowerment across rural North India. The Society runs more than 129 Akal Academies across Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh, educating over 70,000 school students, and also sponsors Eternal University, Baru Sahib. This gives Akal University a strong, trusted feeder network and a deep commitment to rural education.

## Academic Structure
Teaching and research are organized under four faculties: Faculty of Sciences, Faculty of Engineering & Technology, Faculty of Humanities & Social Sciences, and Faculty of Management & Commerce. Ph.D programs are offered in all subjects.

## Why Families & Students Trust Akal University (Our Strengths)
1. Unique heritage location at Takht Sri Damdama Sahib, giving the University a strong identity as "Guru Ki Kashi."
2. Sustained backing of The Kalgidhar Society and its network of 129+ Akal Academies with 70,000+ students.
3. Value-based, disciplined and drug-free residential campus — trusted by parents, especially for daughters, with CCTV security and dedicated counsellors for girl students.
4. Young, research-oriented faculty with doctoral and post-doctoral training from premier institutions such as IITs, IISc, IISER and NIPER.
5. A one-of-its-kind, fully FREE and residential B.A. (Hons.) in Sri Guru Granth Sahib Studies, with comparative religion study.
6. Consistent student success in UGC/CSIR-NET, GATE, CA, PSTET and CTET exams, plus admissions and scholarships to universities abroad (USA, Canada, Italy).
7. Student-centred support: mentor-mentee system, counselling cell, English Language Laboratory, and free coaching for competitive exams (UGC/CSIR-NET, GATE, UPSC-CSE, CA).
8. Generous fee concessions (up to ₹1,25,000/year) for meritorious and needy students — widening access for rural and first-generation learners.
9. Growing national and international academic linkages (e.g., MBA tie-up with Virginia Commonwealth University & University of North Carolina, USA; IIT Bombay for IT courses).
10. Emerging research niches including Green Chemistry and interdisciplinary work in computational and life sciences.

## Looking Ahead (University's 2030 Goals)
Akal University aims to: achieve a high NAAC grade and NIRF ranking; grow intake across all programs, especially from the Malwa region, the Akal Academies network, and the global Punjabi diaspora; build strong research groups with external funding and publications; become a national reference centre for Sri Guru Granth Sahib Studies, Science, Punjabi heritage and the Indian Knowledge System; and run a green, digital, inclusive, drug-free campus as a model for rural higher education.
