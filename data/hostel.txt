## Hostel facilities
Akal University has separate hostels for girls and boys. The hostels are fully furnished and safe, and free from drug abuse.
Students get nutritious vegetarian food at nominal charges. Wardens look after the hostels, and there is 24x7 security with CCTV cameras.
Facilities include a hostel mess and canteen, air-conditioned reading rooms, an indoor gaming hall and an outdoor gym in the playground.

## Hostel and mess fees - AC room vs non-AC (normal) room
Room rent plus mess charges, per student, in a shared room with a common washroom:
- Normal (non-AC) shared room: Rs 40,000
- AC shared room: Rs 45,000
The university lists hostel and mess charges of about Rs 40,000 to Rs 45,000 per year.
Electricity is charged as per actual consumption. Transport charges depend on the boarding place.
Please confirm the final hostel fees, room availability and payment schedule with the admission office.
