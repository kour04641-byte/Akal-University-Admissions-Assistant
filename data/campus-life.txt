## Campus Culture & Values — Source: Official Prospectus 2026-27

## Our Values
Akal University's guiding philosophy rests on "Sanskar Nirman" (character building) — helping students grow into outstanding human beings with sensitivity, courage of conviction, and love for humanity. University motto: "Vidya Veechaaree Taan Parupkaaree" — Contemplate and reflect upon knowledge, and you will become a benefactor to others. Chancellor's message: "Grow in Excellence, Lead with Values."

## Discipline
Every student pledges to follow the University's rules and the principles it is founded on. Sikh students are expected to follow the Sikh way of life and maintain 'Sabat Soorat' (keeping hair unshorn/intact).

## Drug-Free Campus
Akal University maintains zero tolerance for drugs and alcohol of any kind — the entire campus is a drug-free environment.

## Food
Only vegetarian meals are served in the university mess/hostel for all residents.

## Anti-Ragging Policy
Ragging in any form, inside or outside the campus, is strictly prohibited and is a criminal offence (punishable with up to 3 years' imprisonment or a fine up to ₹50,000, or both). Any complaint is investigated within 24 hours, and an FIR is filed where required. As per UGC rules, every student and parent must submit an online anti-ragging affidavit at www.antiragging.in at the time of admission.

## Zero Tolerance for Sexual Harassment
The University maintains a safe, dignified campus for everyone, in compliance with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013, and UGC regulations.

## Dress Code (Day-wise)

Boys:
- Monday & Thursday: Sky Blue shirt, Navy Blue trousers, Navy Blue turban (Sikh students), Navy Blue winter blazer
- Tuesday & Friday: Light Brown shirt, Brown trousers, Kesri (saffron) turban, Navy Blue winter blazer
- Wednesday: White Kurta, White Payjama, Navy Blue turban, Navy Blue winter blazer

Girls:
- Monday & Thursday: Pink Salwar Kameej, White Dupatta, Chocolate winter blazer
- Tuesday & Friday: Lemon Salwar Kameej, White Dupatta, Chocolate winter blazer
- Wednesday: Sea Green Salwar Kameej, White Dupatta, Chocolate winter blazer

Note: Winter blazer is compulsory November–February. During winter, any sweater/cardigan worn must be black only — no other color is permitted.
