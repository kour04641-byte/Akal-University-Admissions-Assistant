## Scholarships & Concessions (Session 2026-27) — Source: Official Prospectus 2026-27

Akal University, through the Kalgidhar Society/Trust, is committed to making education accessible. Multiple scholarship options are available — most students qualify for at least one.

## 1. University Merit Scholarship (based on previous qualifying exam marks)

Undergraduate Courses — Max concession for session 2026-27:
- 95.01% and above: 100% of Tuition Fee waived (up to ₹1,25,000)
- 90.01% – 95.00%: 50% of Tuition Fee waived (up to ₹62,500)
- 85.01% – 90.00%: 15% of Tuition Fee waived (up to ₹18,750)
- 80.01% – 85.00%: 10% of Tuition Fee waived (up to ₹12,500)

Postgraduate Courses — Max concession for session 2026-27:
- 90.01% and above: 100% of Tuition Fee waived (up to ₹80,000)
- 85.01% – 90.00%: 50% of Tuition Fee waived (up to ₹40,000)
- 80.01% – 85.00%: 15% of Tuition Fee waived (up to ₹12,000)
- 70.01% – 80.00%: 10% of Tuition Fee waived (up to ₹8,000)

Conditions:
- Marks must be from the previous qualifying exam with no backlog/reappear.
- Not applicable to B.A.-B.Ed (4-Year ITEP) or Super-30 UPSC coaching batches, and not applicable to MBA (Data Analytics), which is entrance-test based.
- Family income must not exceed ₹8.0 Lakh per annum from all sources.
- Scholarship is adjusted only in the tuition fee.
- Students who reappear in any exam lose scholarship eligibility for that and remaining semesters.
- EWS students not otherwise covered can be considered for financial assistance case-by-case.

## 2. Minority Community Scholarship (Government-sponsored, for Sikh/Muslim/Christian/Jain/Buddhist/Parsi students)
- Merit-cum-Means Scholarship: for students whose parents'/guardian's annual income is below ₹2.5 Lakh.
- Maulana Azad National Fellowship: for M.Phil./Ph.D. studies, income ceiling ₹6.0 Lakh per annum.

## 3. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar SC Post Matric Scholarship
Government of Punjab scheme for Scheduled Caste students domiciled in Punjab (matriculated from Punjab/Chandigarh), with family income not exceeding ₹2.5 Lakh per annum.

## 4. Post Matric Scholarship for OBC Students
Centrally sponsored scheme for Punjab-domicile OBC students, family income not exceeding ₹2.5 Lakh per annum.

## 5. Post Graduate Indira Gandhi Scholarship for Single Girl Child
UGC-sponsored scheme for single girl children admitted to eligible non-professional PG courses.

## 6. Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship
Government of India scheme for meritorious students from low-income families — requires above 80% in Class XII, family income not exceeding ₹8 Lakh per annum.

## 7. Other Scholarships
Various corporate and NGO scholarships are also available periodically; eligible students are encouraged and informed when these open.

## Documents required for scholarship categories 2, 3, and 4 (Minority/SC/OBC)
Scholarship Form with photograph, Freeship Card copy (SC only), Admission Form copy, Notary-attested undertaking/affidavit, Aadhaar card copies of student + parents (linked to mobile), Caste Certificate, Punjab Resident Certificate, Income Certificate from Tehsildar, 10th certificate, last exam marksheet, bank passbook (Aadhaar-linked with IFSC), attendance proforma, gap year certificate (if applicable), 4 passport photos. Minimum 75% lecture attendance is required to claim these scholarships, and students must apply online for Government scholarships by the government's notified deadline themselves.

Our best advice: apply through the Admission Cell as early as possible, and if you're unsure which scholarship you qualify for, the admission office will guide you for free — just call or request a callback.
