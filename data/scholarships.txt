## Akal Online ASAT 2026 - scholarship test (up to 100% scholarship)
The Akal Scholarship Aptitude Test (ASAT) 2026 gives a chance to win up to 100% scholarship on UG and PG programmes.
- Registration is free, and the test date is as per the available slot the student chooses.
- Duration: 30 minutes. Mode: online, proctored. Format: aptitude test (logical reasoning, analytical thinking, quantitative aptitude), multiple-choice questions with negative marking.
- The student needs a laptop or desktop with a working camera and microphone and a reliable internet connection.
- Scholarship by rank as shown on the ASAT page: top ranks can win up to 100% on the full programme fee; Rank 2: 50% scholarship on the full programme fee; Rank 3: 20% scholarship on the full programme fee. All qualifiers get up to 20% scholarship on the 1st semester fee.
- Results are announced within 2-3 days.

## ASAT 2026 - terms and conditions
- To claim the scholarship, the candidate must complete admission within the deadline shared at result declaration, otherwise the scholarship is cancelled.
- The scholarship is valid only for the 2026-27 admission session, applies on the tuition fee of the selected programme, and cannot be transferred, exchanged or redeemed for cash.
- It cannot be combined with other offers, discounts or benefits.
- Malpractice or unfair means can lead to disqualification. Individual scores stay confidential.
- Akal University may change or cancel the scholarship test, dates, terms or structure without prior notice, and its decision is final.
- ASAT registration is on the ASAT 2026 page of the university website: https://auts.ac.in/asat-2026/

## Other financial support and free coaching
The university says it offers merit-based scholarships and need-based financial assistance for economically weaker students. For details of eligibility and amounts, contact the Admission Office on 1800-2020-100 or admission@auts.ac.in.
Enrolled students get free coaching for UPSC Civil Services, CSIR-NET, UGC-NET, SSC and other competitive exams.
